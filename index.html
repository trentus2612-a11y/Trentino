Questions

The things people ask first

Can't see yours? Ask it in the form below — I answer properly, not with a brochure.

You're new — why should I go with you?

Straight answer: Trentino is new, so my prices are lower than they will be once I have a wall of finished work. You get a hand-built site for less than the going rate, and I get a portfolio piece I'm proud to point at. That's the trade, and I'd rather say it plainly than pretend otherwise.

How long does a site take?

A Starter Site is usually 1–2 weeks from the day content is ready. Backend and payments work typically runs 2–4 weeks. The honest bottleneck is almost always getting copy and photos, not the code.

What will it cost me to run each month?

Usually close to nothing. A domain is about $20–$30 a year. Netlify and Supabase both have free tiers that comfortably handle a small business site. Stripe only charges a percentage when you actually take a payment. You pay these directly — I don't mark them up.

Something broke. Do I get charged?

No. If it's something I built and it stops working, fixing it is free. New features or changes of direction get quoted separately so you're never paying for work you didn't ask for.

Can you update an existing site instead of rebuilding?

Sometimes. If it's on a platform I can work with, I'll tell you honestly whether fixing it or rebuilding it is better value. I'd rather give you the cheaper answer than sell you a rebuild you don't need.

Do you write the words and take the photos?

I'll help shape the copy and lay it out so it reads well, and I'll work with photos you provide. Full copywriting or a photo shoot isn't included, but I can point you at people in Adelaide who do it well.

Are you based in Adelaide?

Yes — Adelaide based, and happy to meet in person if that's easier. Remote works fine too; plenty of the process is a shared preview link and a few messages.

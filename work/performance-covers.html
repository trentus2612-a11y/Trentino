Work · Case study

Custom car covers, configured online

Performance Covers Australia makes custom-fit car covers in Adelaide and ships them Australia-wide — indoor or outdoor, any make and model, printed with whatever logo the customer wants. That last part is the catch: no two orders are the same, so every enquiry started with "how much?"

The problem

Every job is a different car, a different fabric, a different colour, and usually a different logo. So nobody could just look up a price — they had to ask, and that meant messages back and forth before anyone knew what the thing cost. Slow for the customer, and hours of unpaid quoting for the business.

What I built

A three-step builder on the front of the site. Pick your make, pick your model, pick the finish — fabric, colour, and your own logo uploaded right there on the page. Three steps in, you've got a price and a summary of exactly what you're ordering. Nobody had to answer a message to make that happen.

Behind it sits a Supabase database holding the orders, the prices and the uploaded logos, with an event log so you can always see what happened to an order and when. Orders land in an admin panel behind a password. Payment goes through Stripe Checkout, so card details never touch the site and the money goes straight into their own Stripe account.

It also installs to a phone and works offline — handy when you're out in a shed with one bar of signal.

What changed

  • Customers price the job themselves. No waiting on a reply, and no quote for anyone to write.
  • Orders arrive finished. Car, fabric, colour and artwork all attached, so there's nothing to chase.
  • Performance Covers runs the admin, not me. Orders land in their panel and they can get at them without asking me.
  • The accounts are theirs. Their Netlify, their Supabase, their Stripe.

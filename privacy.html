Privacy

What happens to your details

Short version: the enquiry form sends me what you type, I use it to reply to you, and that is the end of it. No list, no sharing, no selling. The longer version is below because you deserve specifics rather than a promise.

What I collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, your email address, your business name if you give one, which kind of project you picked, and your message. There is no account to create and nothing is collected in the background.

Where it goes

The form is handled by Netlify Forms. Your submission is stored in my Netlify dashboard and emailed to me at trentus2612@gmail.com. Netlify hosts this site, so their servers also keep standard request logs — the sort of thing every web host records.

What I use it for

Replying to you, quoting your job, and — if we work together — running the project. That is all. I will not add you to a mailing list, and I will not pass your details to anyone else.

Tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. Nothing follows you around after you leave. One thing worth naming honestly: the typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see a request from your browser when the page loads. That is standard on most of the web, but it is not nothing, and you should know.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work get deleted periodically. Project correspondence I keep while the work is live and for a reasonable period afterwards, because clients ask me about their own sites years later and I would rather have the history.

Having it deleted

Email me at trentus2612@gmail.com and ask. I will delete your enquiry and confirm that it is done. You do not have to give a reason.
